On behalf of the Organizing Committee of the 8th IEEE International Conference on Universal Village 2026 (IEEE UV2026), we are pleased to inform you that IEEE UV2026 will be held in a hybrid format on October 17-20, 2026, with the main command in Boston, USA. In view of your distinguished leadership and outstanding contributions to the world, we are delighted to invite you to deliver a keynote speech as a Plenary Speaker at IEEE UV2026.

Universal Village (UV) is a new concept proposed by MIT’s Universal Village Program in 2012. Its goal is to model an ideal future society that pursues harmony between humans and nature through the wise use of technologies. The IEEE UV Conferences, technically co-sponsored by the IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society, call for collective efforts from world-renowned experts across multi-disciplinary fields to collaborate beyond traditional boundaries. The IEEE UV Conferences will also explore systematic methodologies for improving the quality of life by advancing the technology of the UV and its major subsystems, which include Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), Urban Planning and Crowd Management, Smart City Infrastructure, Smart Energy Management, Smart Response Systems for City Emergencies, Smart Environmental Protection, Smart Home and Communities, and Smart Medicine and Healthcare. The International Conferences on UV have achieved great success in China (Beijing, UV2013), Japan (Nagoya, UV2016), and the United States (Boston, UV2014, UV2018, UV2020, UV2022, and UV2024).

The theme of IEEE UV2026 is “Human-Centered AI Transformation for Future Empowerment: Explainable & Human-Intelligible Understanding, Intent-Aware Reasoning & Accountable Decision-Making, Human-Authorized AI Action, and Human-Supervised Reflective Learning.” The conference will have sessions for scientists, students, engineers, entrepreneurs, and government representatives to share their perspectives on various research areas.

Your leadership would encourage this movement among our researchers and help us inspire a revolution in the way mankind lives many generations from now. We would be extremely grateful if you would share your great vision at IEEE UV2026.

Thank you very much for your kind consideration.
